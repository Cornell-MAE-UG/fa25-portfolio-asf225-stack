For my final project in Mechatronics, a mechanical engineering introduction to autonomous robotics, I participate in a 3-person team to design, wire up, code, and assemble together an autonomous robot. The purpose of this project was to place the final robot in an arena and have it navigate its way with the ultimate goal of collecting blocks that were previously placed randomly in the arena. 

My individual contribution to this project was in the form of both robot wiring and repeat iterating of the code to make use of line and color sensors. Our robot used two QTI sensors to stay within the black border of the arena, and color sensors to complete various milestones prior to the competition. I wired up the sensors to the Arduino and ran multiple individual tests to insure that each system worked by itself in the complete configuration. This included making sure the motors for the wheels worked as expected, and monitoring the serial output to make sure I was reading accurate values for the blue and yellow parts of the arena. My teammates then worked with the calibrated sensors to keep the robot on the board and sort out the logic of the one minute competition period.
